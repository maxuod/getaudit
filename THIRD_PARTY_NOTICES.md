# Third-party notices

The project source is licensed under [MIT](LICENSE). Third-party licenses are not replaced by the project's MIT license.

Third-party components retain their upstream licenses. Required legal notices are preserved exactly, including any upstream copyright names and contact information. Product-owner personal information, company information and credentials are not needed for this attribution.

See the [dependency license inventory](docs/legal/DEPENDENCY_LICENSES.md), its [machine-readable record](docs/legal/dependency-licenses.json), and [preserved legal files](docs/legal/licenses/).

The inventory covers 154 locked dependency entries. It preserves 140 unchanged upstream legal files, includes 10 source-backed supplement/provenance files for three packages, and records 16 same-version upstream references. The three declaration/text coverage gaps are addressed. Where upstream omitted a standalone license or copyright notice, the supplement states that fact and does not invent attribution. This inventory covers source dependencies, rather than every library inside separately downloaded native binaries.

## Lightning CSS source notice

Lightning CSS 1.33.0 and its platform variants are declared MPL-2.0. Corresponding upstream source can be obtained from the [v1.33.0 release](https://github.com/parcel-bundler/lightningcss/releases/tag/v1.33.0) or [source tree](https://github.com/parcel-bundler/lightningcss/tree/v1.33.0). When distributing covered executable code, keep the applicable notices and tell recipients how to obtain its source, including covered modifications. Confirm correspondence with the exact binary and preserve applicable rights under the [MPL-2.0 license](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/). Mozilla’s [FAQ](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/FAQ/) explains common distribution cases.

## Before binary distribution

No installer or downloaded runtime binary is included in this source review. Review notices and source availability for the exact runtime/native binaries before distributing an application installer. Electron and Chromium may require notices beyond an npm package license. Keep the applicable notices with any future binary release.

This inventory covers source dependencies and notices; it does not distribute an installer or certify a deployed service. It is an evidence-backed source inventory, not a compliance certification.
